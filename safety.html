Social Media Safety

Learn simple ways to stay safe and protect your personal information online.

Important Safety Rules

🔐 Secure Your Account

Use a strong and unique password for your social media accounts.

🔒 Check Privacy Settings

Keep your profile private and choose who can see your posts.

👤 Be Careful With Strangers

Do not accept unknown people or share personal information with them.

📍 Protect Your Location

Do not share your home address, school, live location or daily routine.

📸 Think Before Posting

Think carefully before sharing photos, videos or comments online.

⚠️ Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links or give your account information to strangers.

Keep Your Personal Information Private

Personal information can be misused by scammers and other harmful people.

  • Keep your phone number private.
  • Do not share your home address.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Keep personal photos safe.

🔑 Use Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication gives your account an extra layer of security.
