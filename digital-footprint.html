Your Digital Footprint

Everything you do online can leave a digital trace. Learn how to manage it safely.

What is a Digital Footprint?

A digital footprint is the information you leave behind when using the internet. Social media posts, comments, photos, searches and online activities can become part of your digital footprint.

📷 Photos

Photos shared online can remain available for a long time.

💬 Comments

Comments and messages can become part of your online identity.

🌐 Web Activity

Websites may record some of your online activities.

Types of Digital Footprints

📢 Active Footprint

Information you intentionally share online, such as posts, comments, likes and photos.

👀 Passive Footprint

Information collected while you browse websites or use online services.

What Creates Your Digital Footprint?

✓ Social media posts

✓ Photos and videos

✓ Online comments

✓ Search history

✓ Online shopping

Think Before You Post

A post that seems funny today may create problems later. Think carefully before sharing anything online.

01. Would I share this publicly?

02. Could this hurt someone?

03. Will I regret posting this later?

How to Manage Your Digital Footprint

Review Old Posts

Check old posts and remove information you no longer want to share.

Use Privacy Settings

Keep your personal information visible only to people you trust.

Think Before Sharing

Consider the future impact before posting personal information.

Search Your Name

Check what information about you is publicly available online.

Build a Positive Digital Identity

Stay aware, protect your information and use social media responsibly.
