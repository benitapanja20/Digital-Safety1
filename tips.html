Social Media Safety Tips

Simple tips to stay safe while using social media.

Important Safety Tips

1. Use a Strong Password

Use a strong and unique password for your social media account.

2. Keep Your Account Private

Use privacy settings to control who can see your posts.

3. Do Not Share Personal Information

Never share your address, phone number, password or OTP publicly.

4. Avoid Unknown Links

Do not open links sent by unknown people.

5. Think Before Posting

Think carefully before posting photos, videos or comments.

6. Block and Report

Block and report accounts that behave suspiciously.
