Online Social Media Risks

Social media is useful, but unsafe use can create different risks. Learn about common online dangers.

Common Online Risks

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages or websites may try to steal your password or personal information.

🔓

Account Hacking

Weak passwords can make your social media account easier to hack.

👤

Fake Profiles

Some people create fake accounts to trick or contact other users.

😔

Cyberbullying

Hurtful messages, comments or posts can cause stress and emotional problems.

📍

Location Sharing

Sharing your live location can reveal where you are to unknown people.

💳

Online Scams

Fake offers, prizes and links may be used to steal money or information.

Remember

Do not share your password, OTP, bank details or personal information with strangers online.

What Should You Do?

  • Use a strong password.
  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Keep your profile private.
  • Block and report suspicious accounts.
  • Tell a trusted person if something feels unsafe.
